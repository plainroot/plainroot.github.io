Support — Uzak

Uzak doesn't have a server, an account system, or a support team — it's small on purpose. If something isn't working, looks wrong, or you just have a question, write directly:

uzayaltiner@gmail.com

It helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. There's nothing to look up on our end beyond what you tell us — the app keeps everything on your phone (see the privacy policy), so we can't see your data to debug it remotely.

A session won't end, or apps stay blocked

Open Uzak and use "Turn off Screen Time" in its Settings. That ends any running session, unshields every app, and hands the Screen Time permission back. No session is meant to run longer than four hours; if one is still up past that, opening Uzak ends it.

Buying or restoring the paid version

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. If a purchase didn't unlock properly, restoring purchases from within the app is usually the fix. For a refund, Apple's own reportaproblem.apple.com is the right place.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it — there's nothing left anywhere else to ask us to remove. If deletion doesn't go through, turn off Screen Time from the app's own Settings first, then try again.
