Support — Uzak

Uzak doesn't have a server, an account system, or a support team — it's small on purpose. If something isn't working, looks wrong, or you just have a question, write directly:

uzayaltiner@gmail.com

It helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your sessions and your city live on your phone (see the privacy policy), so we can't see them to debug it remotely. If the app crashed, or you allowed usage statistics, tap "Copy support IDs" in the app's Settings and paste them into your message; with them we can look up the crash reports and statistics that belong to your install. Nothing from Screen Time is ever among them.

A session won't end, or apps stay blocked

Open Uzak and use "Turn off Screen Time" in its Settings. That ends any running session, unshields every app, and hands the Screen Time permission back. No session is meant to run longer than four hours; if one is still up past that, opening Uzak ends it.

Buying or restoring the paid version

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us; the purchase is then verified through RevenueCat so Pro unlocks. If a purchase didn't unlock properly, "Restore Purchases" in the app's Settings is usually the fix. For a refund, Apple's own reportaproblem.apple.com is the right place.

Turning off usage statistics

Turn off "Share usage statistics" in the app's Settings. From then on nothing more is sent to Amplitude. Crash reports and purchase checks are not covered by that switch; the privacy policy explains what they contain.

Deleting your data

Your sessions and your city are stored on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. If deletion doesn't go through, turn off Screen Time from the app's own Settings first, then try again.

Uzak also sends a small amount of data to outside services: Amplitude (usage statistics, only if you allowed them), RevenueCat (purchase records) and Google Firebase (crash reports and settings). None of them knows your name, so to have that data deleted, open Settings, tap "Copy support IDs", and email what it copied to the address above. Turn off "Share usage statistics" first, so no new statistics are sent afterwards. We delete your Amplitude user and your RevenueCat customer record within 30 days of your email; your purchase stays with Apple, and "Restore Purchases" brings Pro back. For Google (Firebase) we request deletion within 30 days, and Google completes it within up to 180 days.
